The Artisan Hearth

Where Ancient Craft Meets Living Fire

Every morning before dawn, our hearth is stoked with seasoned silver birch and sweet applewood. Our pizzaiolos and chefs hand-stretch 48-hour slow fermented sourdough, dressing each creation with seasonal harvests and cold-pressed valley oils.

At 480°C, the live flame transforms simple bio-dynamic dough into blistered, airy crusts within ninety seconds—locking in crisp smokiness, fragrant herbs, and molten mozzarella.
